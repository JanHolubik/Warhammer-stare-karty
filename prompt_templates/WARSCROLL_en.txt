WARHAMMER PRODUCT CONTENT ENGINE – WARSCROLL CARDS MODE (EN ONLY)

Generate content for only 1 Warhammer product – warscroll cards, datacards, reference cards, or ability cards.

HTML templates already exist.
You will never see them.
Your task is to generate only the values for the placeholders exactly as defined below.

First, internally form a clear understanding of the product based on the product name, EAN, and optional URL.
Never output this internal analysis.
Do not invent uncertain details.
If something is unknown, use a safe and general formulation.

⸻

Main goal

The output must be:
	•	attractive to customers
	•	easy to understand even for beginners
	•	sales-oriented but still factual
	•	specific, trustworthy, and readable
	•	suitable for a modern Warhammer e-commerce store

The customer should quickly understand:
	•	what the cards are
	•	how they are used in gameplay
	•	which faction or army they belong to
	•	how they simplify the game
	•	why they are practical during play
	•	why they are worth buying

⸻

Hard rules
	•	Never generate HTML, CSS, or tags
	•	Do not add comments, explanations, or extra text
	•	Output only the defined placeholders
	•	Keep the exact order of keys
	•	Each key must be filled and used only once
	•	Do not introduce new placeholders
	•	Do not write intro or outro text

Each placeholder must be on a single line:

placeholder: value

Never split values across lines.
Never leave values empty.

⸻

Content rules

Write naturally, clearly, and persuasively.
Avoid filler text and repetition.
Each field must add new information.

Focus on:
	•	faster gameplay
	•	clarity and quick reference
	•	ease of use during play
	•	reducing rulebook lookup
	•	instant access to key information

Highlight:
	•	quick in-game orientation
	•	important information always at hand
	•	smoother turn flow
	•	practical use during matches
	•	usefulness for both beginners and experienced players

Do not describe detailed rules.
Do not invent specific card content.

⸻

Handling uncertainty

Never invent:
	•	number of cards
	•	specific abilities
	•	exact rules
	•	exact contents

Use safe phrasing such as:
	•	“include key rule references”
	•	“allow quick access during gameplay”
	•	“help keep essential information at hand”

⸻

Consistency

The entire output must follow one clear product interpretation.

If the product is a practical gameplay aid that improves speed and clarity, this must be reflected across:
	•	intro
	•	benefits
	•	usage
	•	compatibility
	•	FAQ
	•	recommended products

⸻

Field rules

nazev_produktu: Use a clean product name. Keep official English naming if applicable.

typ_zazitku: Briefly describe what the product is.

hlavni_prinos: One strong sentence combining practicality and value.

hlavni_vyhoda_1–3:
	•	1 = gameplay speed and efficiency
	•	2 = clarity and organization
	•	3 = comfort or army support

cilova_skupina: Must fit naturally after “Ideal choice for …”

frakce_karet: Define faction or use a general description.

edice_karet: Specify if relevant, otherwise keep general.

typ_karet: For example reference cards, ability cards, etc.

pro_koho_jsou_karty: Clearly define target users.

uvodni_text: 2–4 sentences explaining what the product is, its practical benefit, and why it improves gameplay.

vyhoda_1–4: Each must be different. Focus on speed, clarity, comfort, and usability.

sekce_o_kartach_text: Explain why cards are better than searching in a rulebook.

sekce_vyuziti_text: Describe real gameplay situations.

sekce_kompatibilita_text: Describe compatibility in general terms. Do not invent rules.

obsah_radek*: If exact contents are unknown, describe safely.

doporuceny_produkt*:
Use safe general product types such as:
	•	codex / battletome
	•	dice set
	•	measuring tools
	•	miniatures

*_text must explain why the product is relevant.

faq_*:
Realistic questions with short, clear answers.
